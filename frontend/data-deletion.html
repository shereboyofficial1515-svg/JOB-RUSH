Account & Data Deletion

This page explains how to permanently delete your Job Rush account and the personal data associated with it — including if you signed up or logged in with Facebook.

How to request deletion

Option 1 — From your Job Rush account

  1. Log in to Job Rush.
  2. Go to Settings → Account & Security.
  3. Select Delete account and confirm with your password.

If your account was created only through Facebook, Google, or Apple sign-in and you've never set a Job Rush password, use Option 2 or 3 below instead.

Go to Account Settings

Option 2 — Through Facebook (if you signed in with Facebook)

  1. Open Facebook's Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites.
  2. Find Job Rush in your list of active apps.
  3. Select Remove, and choose to also request deletion of your activity/data from the app.

Facebook automatically notifies Job Rush when you do this, and we process the deletion the same way as any other request — no further action is needed from you here.

Option 3 — Email us directly

If you can't log in, email support@jobrush.ng from the address on your account and ask us to delete your account. We'll verify it's really you before acting on the request.

What gets deleted

  • Your name, email address, and phone number (replaced with a permanently anonymized placeholder).
  • Your password.
  • Your profile photo, portfolio images/videos, verification documents, chat attachments, and any other files you uploaded to Job Rush.
  • All of your active login sessions are immediately signed out.
  • Your account is permanently disabled — it can never be logged into again.

What may be retained

A small amount of information may be kept even after deletion, where there's a genuine legal, safety, or platform-integrity reason to do so:

  • Transaction and payment records — kept as required for financial recordkeeping and fraud prevention, independent of your name being attached to them.
  • Messages, reviews, job posts, and dispute history involving other users — these belong to a shared conversation or transaction, so deleting your account doesn't erase the other person's copy of it; your identifying details are removed from your side, but the record itself isn't stripped from people you interacted with.
  • A record that a deletion request occurred — kept for our own audit and abuse-prevention purposes, containing no personal details beyond the fact and date of the request.

This is irreversible

Once your account is deleted, it cannot be recovered or logged back into. If you're not sure yet, consider deactivating your account instead (Settings → Account & Security → Deactivate account) — that hides your profile and signs you out everywhere, but you can log back in any time to restore it.

Questions about this process? Contact support@jobrush.ng or read our Privacy Policy.

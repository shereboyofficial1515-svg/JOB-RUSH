Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. This explains what personal information Job Rush collects, how it's used, who it's shared with, and the choices you have — including how to delete it.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your name, email and/or phone number, and password (stored as a one-way hash, never in readable form).
  • Profile information — professional title, bio, skills, experience, services, pricing, availability, and the state/local government area you select (Job Rush does not access your device's precise GPS location — location is a manual dropdown selection).
  • Photos and videos — your profile picture and any portfolio images/videos you upload.
  • Verification documents — a government ID or similar document, only if you choose to request the Verified badge. Stored privately and only ever visible to you and the reviewing admin.
  • Messages and call metadata — the content of messages you send, chat attachments, and records of when calls happened (audio/video calls are carried over a real-time media connection; we do not record or store the audio/video itself).
  • Payment information — payments are processed by Paystack; Job Rush does not receive or store your card number. We keep records of the transaction (amount, status, timestamps) for your wallet and for legal/financial recordkeeping.
  • Technical information — IP address, device/browser type, and login timestamps, used for account security (e.g. detecting sign-in from a new device) and abuse prevention.
  • Information from third-party sign-in — if you sign in with Google, Facebook, or Apple, we receive the name, email, and a unique identifier they share with us for that purpose. We never receive your password for those services.

How we use your information

  • To operate the core service — creating your profile, connecting hirers and workers, messaging, calls, applications, contracts, and payments.
  • To send notifications you're eligible for (in-app, email, SMS, and push) about account, application, interview, payment, and verification events. See our notification documentation for the full breakdown.
  • To verify identity documents when you request the Verified badge.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and policy violations.
  • To respond to support requests.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers that make Job Rush work, each only for the specific purpose below:

  • Supabase — our database and file storage provider.
  • Paystack — payment processing.
  • LiveKit — the real-time infrastructure that carries audio/video calls.
  • Resend — transactional email delivery.
  • Termii — SMS delivery.
  • Google, Facebook, and Apple — only if you choose to sign in using one of them.
  • Firebase (Google) — push notification delivery to the Job Rush Android app, if you use it.

We may also disclose information where required by law, or to protect the safety of our users or the public.

What other users can see

Your name, profile photo, professional details, general location (state/LGA, not a precise address), portfolio, services, reviews, and badges are visible to other Job Rush users as part of normal platform use. Your password, private contact details, identity documents, and payment details are never shown to other users.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction — review and update your profile information at any time from Settings.
  • Notification preferences — control which channels (email, SMS, push) reach you from Settings.
  • Chat data export — download a copy of your conversations and messages as a JSON file from Settings → Chat.
  • Deletion — request deletion of your account and personal data at any time. See our Data Deletion page for exactly how, and what is and isn't removed.

Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we redact your identifying personal information immediately; some records (payment/transaction history, and messages/reviews/job history that other users are also party to) may be retained in a de-identified form for legal, financial, and platform-integrity reasons. Full detail is on the Data Deletion page.

Security

Passwords are hashed, never stored in plain text. Two-factor authentication is available from Settings → Account & Security. All traffic to Job Rush is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Children's privacy

Job Rush is a work and hiring platform intended for users who are at least 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the "last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in-app.

Contact us

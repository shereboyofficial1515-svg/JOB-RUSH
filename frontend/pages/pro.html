A few things to know

Does PRO replace the Verified badge?

No. Verified is free and requires admin approval of your identity documents. PRO is a paid visibility subscription. You can have neither, either, or both — they're independent.

Will PRO guarantee me jobs?

No. PRO improves your visibility in relevant searches — it does not override relevance, ratings, or your profile completeness, and it doesn't guarantee jobs, customers, or income.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancelling stops future renewals — you keep PRO for the rest of the period you've already paid for, then it won't auto-renew.
